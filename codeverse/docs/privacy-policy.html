CodeVerse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-24

CodeVerse is built for kids, so we designed it to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

Nothing that leaves your device. CodeVerse has no account system, no login, and no online social features. The only piece of information you give the app is a nickname, which is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

All progress — XP, coins, levels completed, stars, streaks, badges, and cosmetic items — is stored locally on your device using on-device storage (Hive). It is never uploaded to a server, because CodeVerse doesn't have one.

What we don't do

  • No advertising or ad networks.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • No third-party data sharing.
  • No online leaderboards or chat — the "leaderboard" in CodeVerse is a personal rank ladder based only on your own progress.

Purchases

If a parent chooses to unlock PRO content, the purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. CodeVerse never sees or stores payment information — it only receives a yes/no entitlement result from Google Play's billing system.

Parental controls

Purchases and the Parent Dashboard are protected by a simple arithmetic "parental gate" intended to keep young children from triggering them by accident. This is a friction check, not an identity or age-verification system.

Children's privacy

CodeVerse is designed in line with the spirit of children's privacy laws (such as COPPA): we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, child or adult, because the app simply doesn't ask for or transmit any.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this document will be updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app's listed support contact on its Google Play Store page.
